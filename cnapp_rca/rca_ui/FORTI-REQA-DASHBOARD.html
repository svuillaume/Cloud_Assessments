Methodology & Data Sources

🔒

Risk Calculation: FAIR framework (Factor Analysis of Information Risk), computed via FortiCNAPP's Risk Quantitative Exposure Calculator (Cloud Risk → Financial Risk → FortiCNAPP ROI). The reference customer (620 workloads, 45 developers) carries a Threat Event Frequency and Exposure score derived from 10 high-fidelity alerts, 55 host-exposure findings, 38 identity-risk findings, 42 secrets, and 30 misconfigurations, against a $2.2M loss-magnitude assumption per breach event — yielding an Annual Loss Expectancy (ALE) of $858K. Selecting a different workload tier scales all finding counts and ALE proportionally to this baseline, since a larger cloud footprint implies a proportionally larger risk surface.

Investment & Pricing: FortiCNAPP standard workload-protection pricing ($400/workload/yr for 250–2,499 workloads, $380/yr for 2,500–4,999, $350/yr for 5,000+) plus Code Security ($650/developer/yr, 20-developer minimum order quantity). All prices default to list; the Sales Discount inputs in the Analysis Basis card apply a custom discount to both lines and recompute investment and ROI accordingly.

Deployment Tiers: Tier 1 (CSPM + CIEM + CWPP Agentless + DSPM, 20/40/55% risk reduction over 3 years, 95% confidence), Tier 2 (Tier 1 + CWPP Agentless and Agent, 25/45/60% reduction, 85% confidence), Tier 3 (Tier 2 + Code Security + Pentesting, 30/50/65% reduction, 70% confidence — lower confidence reflects the larger scope and coordination required for full-stack deployment).

MTTR / Ops Benefit: Based on critical-incident volume scaled to the workload tier, assuming FortiCNAPP reduces mean time to remediate from an industry-baseline 36 hours to 5 hours, at a combined $285/hr analyst + incident-response cost.

Data Sources: Verizon DBIR 2024 (breach frequency), Ponemon Institute 2023 (breach costs), Forrester TEI 2022 (operational savings), FAIR Institute (methodology), FortiCNAPP documentation (pricing, remediation rates).

Confidence Note: All three tiers show positive ROI within 4–7 months' payback at list price, across every workload tier tested (250–7,500 workloads). Actual payback and ROI depend on the workload tier, deployment tier, and any sales discount applied.
